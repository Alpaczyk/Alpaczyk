I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and high-performance
computing — with a focus on **LLM inference**, **GPU memory behaviour** and
**performance engineering**.

## Selected Work

**[Hybrid LLM Inference — Performance & Cache Memory](https://github.com/Alpaczyk/Understanding-Performance-and-Cache-Memory-in-Hybrid-LLM-Inference)** · *MSc thesis*

Benchmarking vLLM and SGLang across hybrid attention/SSM architectures:
throughput, TTFT/TPOT latency and GPU cache-memory behaviour under realistic
serving workloads, measured on NVIDIA DGX B200.

**[GPT from Scratch](https://github.com/Alpaczyk/neetcode-gpt)**

A transformer implemented from the ground up in PyTorch — multi-head and
grouped-query attention, KV caching, RMSNorm, a character-level tokenizer,
training loop and autoregressive generation.

**[AI Football Commentary](https://github.com/Alpaczyk/AiFootballCommentary)** · *BSc thesis*

Live, GPT-3-generated audio commentary for Google Research Football matches —
preserved as a 2023 time capsule of LLM + TTS integration.
